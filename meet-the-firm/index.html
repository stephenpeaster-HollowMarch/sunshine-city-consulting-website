Meet the Firm

Sunshine City Consulting, LLC is a one-person firm: Stephen Peaster, working directly with business owners and their teams.

Stephen Peaster

Stephen is a sales and use tax consultant with 8+ years in state and local tax. He holds the CMI – Sales Tax designation from the Institute for Professionals in Taxation (Cert. #1389) and specializes in construction sales and use tax — the tax questions that come with construction and real property improvements.

His work covers the full range of sales-tax matters: multi-jurisdictional nexus analysis, exposure assessments, audit defense, voluntary disclosure agreements, reverse audits, and penalty abatement. His clients have come from e-commerce, software, manufacturing, and food and beverage.

Sunshine City Consulting, LLC is based in St. Petersburg, Florida. The firm works directly with business owners and their teams, bringing an outside perspective to complex decisions. The aim is simple: honest advice, offered so business owners can move forward with confidence.

The idea behind this firm is simple: no one should have to carry the weight of a business alone.
