Meet the Firm

Sunshine City Consulting, LLC is a one-person firm: Stephen Peaster, working directly with business owners and their teams.

Stephen Peaster

Stephen Peaster is the sales and use tax consultant behind Sunshine City Consulting, LLC in St. Petersburg, Florida. He began his tax career in 2018 and holds the CMI designation in sales tax from the Institute for Professionals in Taxation. His work includes helping businesses determine where to register, reviewing how tax applies to purchases and sales, and responding to state audits. He has a particular interest in construction and real property improvements, and his experience spans software, manufacturing, and food and beverage.

Stephen served as a noncommissioned officer in the Florida Army National Guard from 2013 to 2022, earning the Army Achievement Medal and the National Defense Service Medal.

His work covers the full range of sales-tax matters: multi-jurisdictional nexus analysis, multi-state taxability analysis, exposure assessments, notice resolution, audit defense, voluntary disclosure agreements, reverse audits, and penalty abatement. His experience spans e-commerce, software, manufacturing, and food and beverage.

Sunshine City Consulting is a firm based in St. Petersburg, Florida, set up to help businesses work through the questions that come with running and growing a company. It is intended to work directly with business owners and their teams and to bring an outside perspective to complex decisions. The aim is simple: honest advice, offered so business owners can move forward with confidence.

The idea behind this firm is simple: no one should have to carry the weight of a business alone.
