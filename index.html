About the firm

Sunshine City Consulting is a firm based in St. Petersburg, Florida, set up to help businesses work through the questions that come with running and growing a company. It is intended to work directly with business owners and their teams and to bring an outside perspective to complex decisions. The aim is simple: honest advice, offered so business owners can move forward with confidence.

The idea behind this firm is simple: no one should have to carry the weight of a business alone.

Services at a glance

Sales and use tax consulting, in eight areas. Each page explains what the service covers and who it is for.

Nexus analysis

Find out where your business may need to register and who is responsible for collecting tax.

Multi-state taxability analysis

Find out whether your products or services are taxable in each state where you sell them.

Exposure assessments

Estimate potential sales and use tax exposure by state and period.

Notice resolution

Work out what a state tax notice means, and which response it calls for.

Audit defense

Get help responding to a state audit, from the first information request to the findings.

Voluntary disclosure

Assess whether voluntary disclosure is an option for past tax exposure.

Reverse audits

Check whether your business paid more sales or use tax than it should have.

Penalty abatement

Determine whether the facts support a request to reduce or remove a penalty.
