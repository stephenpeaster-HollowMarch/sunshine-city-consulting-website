About the firm

Sunshine City Consulting is a firm based in St. Petersburg, Florida, set up to help businesses work through the questions that come with running and growing a company. It is intended to work directly with business owners and their teams and to bring an outside perspective to complex decisions. The aim is simple: honest advice, offered so business owners can move forward with confidence.

The idea behind this firm is simple: no one should have to carry the weight of a business alone.

Services at a glance

Sales and use tax consulting, in six areas. Each page explains what the service covers and who it is for.

Nexus analysis

Multi-jurisdictional nexus review: where a business has sales-tax obligations.

Exposure assessments

Quantifying past sales-tax exposure so a business knows what it owes.

Audit defense

Support through a state sales-tax audit, from notice to resolution.

Voluntary disclosure

Voluntary disclosure agreements to limit lookback and penalties.

Reverse audits

Recovery of sales and use tax a business overpaid.

Penalty abatement

Requests for relief from sales-tax penalties.

How to engage

A typical engagement starts with a conversation:

  1. Email the firm — a brief description of the matter, the state or states involved, and the timeline.
  2. Discuss the matter and scope — what is at issue, what work is needed, and what it will take.
  3. Agree the engagement and start work — scope and terms confirmed, then the work begins.
