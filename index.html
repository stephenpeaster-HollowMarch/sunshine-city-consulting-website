About the firm

Sunshine City Consulting is based in St. Petersburg, Florida. The firm exists to help businesses work through the questions that come with running and growing a company. We work directly with business owners and their teams, bringing an outside perspective to complex decisions. The goal is straightforward: honest advice, so business owners can move forward with confidence.

Nobody should have to carry the weight of a business alone. That's the idea behind this firm.

Services at a glance

Sales and use tax consulting, across eight areas. Each page lays out what the service covers and who it's for.

Nexus analysis

Figure out where your business may need to register and who is responsible for collecting the tax.

Multi-state taxability analysis

Find out if what you sell is taxable in each state where you sell it.

Exposure assessments

Estimate how much sales and use tax exposure you might be facing, by state and period.

Notice resolution

Make sense of a state tax notice — what it's telling you and what you need to do about it.

Audit defense

Get support through a state audit, start to finish, from the first information request all the way through to the findings.

Voluntary disclosure

Look at whether voluntary disclosure is an option for past tax exposure.

Reverse audits

Find out if your business paid more sales or use tax than it should have.

Penalty abatement

Look at the facts and decide whether they support a request to reduce or remove a penalty.
