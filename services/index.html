Services

Start with the question your business is facing. Maybe you need to register in a new state, launch a new product or service, or respond to a state tax notice. Look through the summaries below to find the service that fits, then reach out to Stephen with the state involved and any deadline you're working against.

Nexus analysis

Find out where your business may need to register and who has to collect the tax. If you sell through your own website and a marketplace, the answer can differ depending on the sales channel. Stephen looks at your sales and the rules that apply before you register or start collecting.

Multi-state taxability analysis

Find out whether what you sell is taxable in each state where you sell it. Stephen looks at your products or services, how each state classifies them, and which exemptions might apply. What you get back is a state-by-state picture of where tax applies, at what rate, and what may be exempt.

Exposure assessments

Get an estimate of potential sales and use tax exposure by state and period. If a registration was missed or purchases went untaxed, Stephen goes through the available sales and purchase records and estimates what may be owed.

Notice resolution

Figure out what a state tax notice means and what kind of response it calls for. Stephen reads through the notice, the periods and amounts involved, and the deadline. Then he helps you decide how to respond — whether that means audit defense, penalty abatement, or something else.

Audit defense

Get help working through a state audit, from the first information request all the way to the findings. Stephen starts by looking at what the auditor requested, which periods are under review, and when your response is due.

Voluntary disclosure

Find out whether voluntary disclosure is an option for past tax exposure. Stephen looks at the periods involved, whether tax was collected, and any prior contact with the state before he recommends what to do next.

Reverse audits

Check whether your business overpaid sales or use tax. Stephen compares invoices, exemption records, and use tax payments against the rules that apply to the transaction.

Penalty abatement

Find out whether the facts support asking a state to reduce or remove a penalty. Stephen looks at the notice, the timeline, and what caused the missed obligation. If there's a basis for relief, he puts together the request with the supporting facts.
