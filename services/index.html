Services

Sales and use tax consulting, in six areas. Each service page explains what it covers, who it is for, and what the work looks like.

The firm's launch scope is sales and use tax consulting only. If the team grows, more services may be added here — nothing is promised before it exists.

Nexus analysis

Multi-jurisdictional nexus review: where a business has sales-tax obligations. The review maps a business's footprint — where it sells, where its people and property are, and where it crosses economic thresholds — against each state's rules, so the business knows where it must register, collect, and file.

Exposure assessments

Quantifying past sales-tax exposure so a business knows what it owes. The assessment looks backward at uncollected or unpaid tax and estimates what it could cost in tax, interest, and penalties — so the business can decide what to do about it with real numbers.

Audit defense

Support through a state sales-tax audit, from notice to resolution. The work covers reviewing the notice and the auditor's requests, preparing and reviewing records, responding to findings, and working toward the best resolution — including protest or appeal where warranted.

Voluntary disclosure

Voluntary disclosure agreements to limit lookback and penalties. A business with past exposure comes forward to a state on agreed terms — usually a limited lookback period and reduced or waived penalties — instead of waiting to be found.

Reverse audits

Recovery of sales and use tax a business overpaid. The review examines tax already paid — purchases, exemptions, and exclusions — to find amounts the business should not have paid, and prepares the refund claims.

Penalty abatement

Requests for relief from sales-tax penalties. When a business faces penalties after an assessment, audit, or late filing, the request sets out the grounds for relief — usually reasonable cause — and asks the state to reduce or remove the penalties.
