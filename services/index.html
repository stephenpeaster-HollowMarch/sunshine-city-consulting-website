Services

Start with the question your business is facing. You might need to register in a new state, put a new product or service on the market, or respond to a state tax notice. Use the summaries below to find the service that fits, then contact Stephen with the state involved and any deadline.

Nexus analysis

Find out where your business may need to register and who is responsible for collecting tax. If you sell through your own website and a marketplace, the answer can differ by sales channel. Stephen reviews your sales and the rules that apply before you register or collect.

Multi-state taxability analysis

Find out whether your products or services are taxable in each state where you sell them. Stephen reviews what you sell, how each state classifies it, and which exemptions may apply. The result is a state-by-state view of where tax applies, at what rate, and what may be exempt.

Exposure assessments

Estimate potential sales and use tax exposure by state and period. If a registration was missed or purchases went untaxed, Stephen reviews the available sales and purchase records and estimates what may be owed.

Notice resolution

Work out what a state tax notice means, and which response it calls for. Stephen reviews the notice, the periods and amounts involved, and the deadline. He then helps you decide on the response — whether the matter belongs with audit defense, penalty abatement, or another route.

Audit defense

Get help responding to a state audit, from the first information request to the findings. Stephen starts with what the auditor requested, which periods are under review, and when the response is due.

Voluntary disclosure

Assess whether voluntary disclosure is an option for past tax exposure. Stephen reviews the periods involved, whether tax was collected, and any prior contact with the state before recommending next steps.

Reverse audits

Check whether your business paid more sales or use tax than it should have. Stephen compares invoices, exemption records, and use tax payments with the rules that apply to the transaction.

Penalty abatement

Determine whether the facts support a request to reduce or remove a penalty. Stephen reviews the notice, the timeline, and the reason for the missed obligation. If there is a basis for relief, he prepares the request with the supporting facts.
