Reverse audits

Check whether your business paid more sales or use tax than it should have.

← All services

What it is

Stephen compares invoices, exemption records, and use tax payments with the rules that apply to the transaction.

Who needs it

Businesses that have been paying sales or use tax for years without a systematic check of exemptions and exclusions. It is especially worth doing after a change in what the business buys, after an expansion into new states, or before a sale.

What Stephen does

He compares invoices, exemption records, and use tax payments with the rules that apply to the transaction, quantifies the overpayments, and prepares the refund claims.
