Reverse audits

Find out if your business paid more sales or use tax than it should have.

← All services

What it is

We compare invoices, exemption records, and use tax payments against the rules that apply to the transaction.

Who needs it

If your business has been paying sales or use tax for years without a systematic check of exemptions and exclusions, that's worth fixing. It matters even more after you've changed what you're buying, moved into new states, or when you're getting ready to sell.

What we do

We go through invoices, exemption records, and use tax payments, match them against the rules that apply to the transaction, figure out how much was overpaid, and put together the refund claims.
