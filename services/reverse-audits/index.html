Reverse audits

Recovery of sales and use tax a business overpaid.

← All services

What it is

A reverse audit is a review of tax a business has already paid, to find amounts it overpaid — tax paid on exempt purchases, on transactions that were not taxable, or on purchases where the wrong rate or base was used. Where overpayments are found, the business claims them back from the vendor or the state.

Who needs it

Businesses that have been paying sales or use tax for years without a systematic check of exemptions and exclusions. It is especially worth doing after a change in what the business buys, after an expansion into new states, or before a sale.

What Stephen does

He examines purchase records, invoices, and exemption certificates, tests them against each state's rules, quantifies the overpayments, and prepares the refund claims — to the vendor where the vendor collected the tax, and to the state where the business paid it directly.
