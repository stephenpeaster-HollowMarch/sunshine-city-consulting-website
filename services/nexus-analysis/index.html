Nexus analysis

Multi-jurisdictional nexus review: where a business has sales-tax obligations.

← All services

What it is

Nexus is the connection between a business and a state that obligates the business to register, collect, and remit sales tax there. A nexus analysis reviews where a business's activities create those obligations — physical presence, economic thresholds on sales or transactions, marketplace facilitator rules, and the activities of employees, contractors, and affiliates. The result is a clear map: where the business must be registered, where it does not yet need to be, and what comes next.

Who needs it

Businesses selling into new states, growing online sales, opening offices or warehouses, using remote workers or contractors across state lines, or selling through marketplaces. It is also the right first step for any business that has never checked where it owes sales tax.

What Stephen does

He reviews how and where the business operates — sales by state, channels, headcount, property, and third-party relationships — and applies each state's nexus rules to that footprint. He then lays out which registrations are required, in what order, and what the business should do about states where it has past exposure.
