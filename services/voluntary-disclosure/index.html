Voluntary disclosure

Assess whether voluntary disclosure is an option for past tax exposure.

← All services

What it is

Stephen reviews the periods involved, whether tax was collected, and any prior contact with the state before recommending next steps.

Who needs it

Businesses with past exposure — sales in states where they were not registered, or tax that went uncollected — that want to regularize their position. It is often the right move before a sale, a financing, or an expansion that would surface the exposure anyway.

What Stephen does

He first measures the exposure, then weighs whether a VDA is the right route for that state. If it is, he prepares the disclosure, submits it, and negotiates the terms — the lookback period, the amounts, and the penalty treatment — with the state.
