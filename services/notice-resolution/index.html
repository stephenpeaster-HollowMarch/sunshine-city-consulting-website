Notice resolution

Make sense of a state tax notice — what it's telling you and what you need to do about it.

← All services

What it is

We read through the notice, the periods and amounts involved, and the deadline. Then we help you decide how to respond — whether that means audit defense, penalty abatement, or something else.

Who needs it

If your business has received a notice from a state tax authority — an assessment, a demand for payment, a registration notice, a penalty notice, or an audit notice — and you're not sure what it means or what happens next, this is the right first step. The deadline is already running.

What we do

We read the notice front to back, figure out what the state is asking for, which periods and amounts are on the table, and when a response is due. Then we lay out your options and help put the response together. If the notice turns out to be the start of an audit, the work continues as audit defense.
