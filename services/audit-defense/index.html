Audit defense

Support through a state sales-tax audit, from notice to resolution.

← All services

What it is

A state sales-tax audit is a review of a business's sales, purchases, and records to check whether the right amount of tax was collected and paid. Audit defense is having someone who knows the rules in the room — from the first notice through the auditor's findings to the final determination.

Who needs it

Any business under audit, or one that has received an audit notice, an information request, or a proposed assessment. The earlier support starts, the more control the business has over how the audit goes.

What Stephen does

He reviews the notice and the auditor's requests, helps prepare and review the records, responds to the auditor's findings, and works toward the best resolution — including protest or appeal where the facts support it.
