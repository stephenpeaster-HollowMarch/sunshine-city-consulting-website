Multi-state taxability analysis

Find out whether your products or services are taxable in each state where you sell them.

← All services

What it is

Stephen reviews what you sell, how each state classifies it, and which exemptions may apply. The result is a state-by-state view of where tax applies, at what rate, and what may be exempt.

Who needs it

Businesses selling into more than one state, launching a new product or service, or selling things whose tax treatment changes by state — software and digital goods, food and beverages, manufactured products. His experience spans e-commerce, software, manufacturing, and food and beverage, where the same product can be taxed differently from one state to the next. It is also the right check before setting prices or charging tax on a new product. Construction businesses face the same question with materials, labor, and fixtures, which can be taxed differently from one state to the next.

What Stephen does

He reviews your product and service list, how each item is sold, and where your customers are, then applies each state's taxability rules — rates, exemptions, and product classifications — to that list. He lays out, state by state, what is taxable, what is exempt, and what needs a closer look.
