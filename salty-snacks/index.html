SALTy Snacks

Stephen's LinkedIn series on sales and use tax developments and what they mean for businesses.

The list below gathers the 14 posts published so far — October 1 to November 20, 2026, newest first — with a short summary of each and a link to the full post on LinkedIn. New posts will be added here as they publish.

November 20, 2026

Texas proposes to end sales tax on streaming and data processing services to include SaaS

On back-to-back days, outgoing Texas Comptroller Don Huffines proposed narrowing the sales-tax base: Rule 3.313 would end the tax on streaming, and Rule 3.308 would take hosted technology services and SaaS out of taxable data processing, unless the offering includes taxable data processing, such as processing a customer’s own data in Texas. The chief revenue estimator projects about $290 million in lost state general revenue in fiscal 2027.

Read the full post on LinkedIn

November 17, 2026

Illinois and Utah's targeted advertising (i.e., digital ads) tax effective January 1, 2027. Litigation continues across the states.

Illinois's 10% targeted advertising tax and Utah's 4.7% tax both take effect January 1, 2027. Litigation continues: Maryland's digital advertising tax was struck down in August, Illinois is already in court, and Utah faces a constitutional challenge.

Read the full post on LinkedIn

November 12, 2026

Texas draws the line on data-center power. A power plant is not exempt data center equipment just because it is adjacent to and primarily supporting a data center.

The Texas Comptroller concluded power-generation assets serving a qualifying data center do not qualify for the project exemption just because they sit alongside it — though the same equipment may qualify for the manufacturing exemption when used to manufacture electricity for sale.

Read the full post on LinkedIn

November 10, 2026

Maryland's multiple points of use exemptions can be used on a blanket basis beginning January 1, 2027.

Beginning January 1, 2027, an authorized buyer issues one vendor-level multiple points of use certificate to each vendor, replacing the transaction-by-transaction approach in effect since July 1, 2025.

Read the full post on LinkedIn

November 9, 2026

Oklahoma extends exempt-entity sales tax treatment to contractors' qualified purchases.

Effective November 1, 2026, SB 44 extends an exempt entity's sales-tax treatment to contractors and subcontractors buying tangible personal property or services to perform a contract with that entity.

Read the full post on LinkedIn

November 6, 2026

Texas: Marketplace fees are one step from nontaxable

The Comptroller formally proposed removing the provision that taxes marketplace fees as data processing. The current rule stays in effect until the change is final, so sellers should keep remitting for now.

Read the full post on LinkedIn

October 23, 2026

Missouri says video-game skins aren't taxable — and the marketplace isn't a facilitator

Missouri DOR's Letter Ruling LR 8408 held that transfers of Counter-Strike digital cosmetic items on a peer-to-peer marketplace aren't taxable sales, so the platform has no marketplace-facilitator duties.

Read the full post on LinkedIn

October 15, 2026

D.C. delays its sales tax hike — but rental cars and occupancy get higher rates.

D.C.'s FY2027 Budget Support Act holds the general rate at 6% for FY2027 instead of moving to 7%, while the rental car tax rises to 11% and an additional 1% lodging tax becomes permanent.

Read the full post on LinkedIn

October 9, 2026

Texas Supreme Court: franchise tax is sourced to where title transfers.

In NuStar Energy v. Hancock, the court held franchise-tax receipts from tangible personal property are sourced where the buyer takes possession and control in Texas — "what matters is the customer's location when the seller surrenders the goods."

Read the full post on LinkedIn

October 8, 2026

Netflix's Colorado sales tax refund argument dismissed by CO Supreme Court.

Netflix and the Colorado DOR settled, and on July 22 the Colorado Supreme Court dismissed the case with prejudice — so the 2025 Court of Appeals decision stands: streaming subscriptions are "corporeal" tangible personal property under Colorado's 1935 statute.

Read the full post on LinkedIn

October 7, 2026

Washington tiers its B&O tax for service businesses.

As of October 1, 2025, Washington applies a three-tier service B&O structure: 1.5% under $1 million, 1.75% from $1 million to $4.999 million, and 2.1% at $5 million or more of Washington-sourced services revenue, measured in the prior year.

Read the full post on LinkedIn

October 5, 2026

California's SaaS tax rules take shape

The CDTFA released its September 1 discussion paper with draft emergency regulations implementing SB 122, which will treat most prewritten software and SaaS as taxable tangible personal property starting January 1, 2027.

Read the full post on LinkedIn

October 2, 2026

New Jersey freezes new letter rulings for 60 days

New Jersey froze letter-ruling requests for 60 days effective October 1; written requests may still receive non-binding general guidance, which carries no ruling's protection in an audit.

Read the full post on LinkedIn

October 1, 2026

Texas moves to end sales tax on marketplace fees.

Comptroller Huffines signed an executive order directing his agency to amend Rule 3.330 and remove marketplace and platform fees from taxable data processing services.
